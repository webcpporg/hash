== Changelog

What changed in hash in each version of webcpp, newest first, one line
per change. "Unreleased" is what the next version brings. The release notes
of every version are on webcpp's index page.

[#hash-changelog-unreleased]
=== Unreleased

// tag::unreleased[]
* *Breaking change:* Changed the public names: hkdf's input_key_material, pseudorandom_key, application_info, output_key_material and salt, hkdf_max_application_info_size, scrypt_parameters' cost, block_size and parallelism (from n, r, p) and argon2_parameters::memory_kibibytes.
* *Breaking change:* Changed HKDF's overloads: `+hkdf(out, {}, {}, {})+`, `+async_hkdf(ex, out, salt, {}, {}, token)+` and `+{vec}+` inputs are ambiguous; pass x, an empty span or `+byte_pieces{...}+`.
* Added the library: Boost.Hash2's algorithms and HMAC under webcpp::hash's names, keccak_256 (Ethereum's Keccak-256 over XKCP), blake3 (keyed, key derivation, extendable output), every result `+[[nodiscard]]+`.
* Added HKDF (RFC 5869) and PBKDF2 (RFC 8018) on hash2, argon2id (RFC 9106), scrypt and argon2's Argon2i and Argon2id on sodium; keyed calls set DIT on arm64 Apple and Linux.
* Added HKDF over byte pieces (hkdf_extract, hkdf_expand, hkdf and their async forms), with no cap and no heap of hash's own on every backend.
* Added backends::openssl (OpenSSL 3.5.9; native, wasip2, wasip3) and backends::botan (Botan 3.14.1, with exceptions), named in the call: their digests, MACs, key derivations and, on Botan, bcrypt.
* Added backends::sodium (HKDF, BLAKE2b with its options, keyed BLAKE2b, SipHash, blake2b_subkey, SHA-2, SHA-3, SHAKE, TurboSHAKE, HMAC) and backends::bearssl (HKDF, MD5, SHA-1, SHA-2, MD5-SHA1, SHAKE, HMAC).
* Added async.hpp, async_digest, async_hmac, async_hkdf_extract, async_hkdf_expand, async_hkdf, async_pbkdf2, async_argon2id, async_argon2 and async_scrypt over webcpp::asio, and backends::webcrypto, the browser's WebCrypto, for them on Emscripten.
* Added each function's default backend, the first of hash2, xkcp, blake3 and sodium that has it; the guide, doc/capabilities.json, measurements, allocation tests and a keccak_224-512 proposal to Boost.Hash2.
* Added the families digest_of, hasher, mac and argon2 over digests, macs and kdfs tags on hash2, xkcp (Keccak, SHA-3, SHAKE, SP 800-185, KangarooTwelve, TurboSHAKE), blake3 and sodium, and error::invalid_state.
// end::unreleased[]
