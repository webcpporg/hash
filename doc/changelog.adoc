== Changelog

What changed in hash in each version of webcpp, newest first, one line
per change. "Unreleased" is what the next version brings. The release notes
of every version are on webcpp's index page.

[#hash-changelog-unreleased]
=== Unreleased

// tag::unreleased[]
* Added the library: Boost.Hash2's algorithms and HMAC under webcpp::hash's names, keccak_256 (Ethereum's Keccak-256 over XKCP), blake3 (keyed, key derivation, extendable output), every result `+[[nodiscard]]+`.
* Added HKDF (RFC 5869) and PBKDF2 (RFC 8018) over every Boost.Hash2 hmac on backends::hash2, and on backends::openssl, OpenSSL 3.5.9 through /webcpp/hash//openssl (native, wasip2, wasip3), refusing lengths past its int.
* Added argon2id (RFC 9106, one lane) with libsodium's three presets, scrypt (RFC 7914), and argon2's Argon2i and Argon2id with one lane, on backends::sodium.
* Added backends::sodium's HKDF over HMAC-SHA-256/512, BLAKE2b with key, salt, personalisation and length, SipHash-2-4, SipHash-2-4x, blake2b_subkey, SHA-256/512, SHA3-256/512, SHAKE, TurboSHAKE with its domain byte, and HMAC-SHA-256/512.
* Added backends::bearssl's HKDF over MD5, SHA-1 and SHA-2 (hkdf alone), and its MD5, SHA-1, SHA-2, MD5-SHA1, SHAKE and HMAC as digests and MACs, named in the call.
* Added the default backend, hash2 for key derivation and sodium for argon2id, scrypt, blake2b_subkey and argon2, and doc/capabilities.json naming each function's default and backends::webcrypto's asynchronous-only cells.
* Added async.hpp, async_digest, async_hmac, async_hkdf, async_pbkdf2, async_argon2id, async_argon2 and async_scrypt over webcpp::asio, and backends::webcrypto, the browser's WebCrypto, for them on Emscripten.
* Added the measured tables (doc/measurements.*) from release builds, a reference of 2026-11-16 that decides nothing, and allocation tests: none in hash's code.
* Added the guide: chapters per family and per use case, an example per section, generated tables and llms.txt, and a Boost.Hash2 proposal of keccak_224 to keccak_512.
* Added the families digest_of, hasher, mac and argon2 over digests, macs and kdfs tags on hash2, xkcp (Keccak, SHA-3, SHAKE, SP 800-185, KangarooTwelve, TurboSHAKE), blake3 and sodium, and error::invalid_state.
// end::unreleased[]
